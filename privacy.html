YOUR RECORDING. YOUR CONTROL.

Privacy Policy.

Local prototype · Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy describes the current Cue demonstration running on your device. It explains what happens when you select a recording, enter a transcript, or review an assessment.

Your call is processed in your browser.

Cue does not send your recording or transcript to a transcription server. The website does contact external services to load fonts and speech-recognition software, as explained below.

Cue saves your light or dark mode preference in browser storage so it can apply the same appearance across pages and visits.

01 / Information you choose to use

When you select a file, Cue reads its audio, filename, size, and duration. It processes that audio to create a transcript and an assessment. You can also enter or edit transcript text without a recording.

Speaker labels, action-checklist answers, completed steps, warning feedback, and optional feedback notes are used to display your current assessment. No account, email address, or phone number is required.

02 / How that information is used

Recordings are decoded and transcribed on your device. Transcript rules identify possible warning signs and schemes. Recording-quality checks examine the audio signal. Feedback is for your review and saved reports; it does not train or retrain a model.

The current prototype has no analytics, advertising, payment processing, or cloud account storage. Cue does not sell your recordings or transcripts or send them to an advertising service.

03 / External downloads and links

Google Fonts provides the website fonts. When you start audio transcription, the browser downloads the speech engine from jsDelivr and model files from Hugging Face and their delivery infrastructure. These services receive ordinary request information, such as your IP address, the requested file, and browser headers. Cue does not attach your call recording, transcript, checklist, or feedback to those requests.

Their handling of request data is governed by their own policies: Google Privacy Policy ↗, jsDelivr Privacy Policy ↗, and Hugging Face Privacy Policy ↗. Learn more about Google Fonts and privacy ↗.

If you open an external guidance link, that website receives your visit and its own privacy policy applies. Cue does not include your analysis in those links.

04 / Storage, cookies, and clearing a session

Cue keeps your current call analysis and choices in the active browser session. It does not intentionally save call content, labels, or feedback in browser local storage or a server database, and it does not set its own tracking cookies. The browser may cache downloaded model, software, and font files.

Select Reset to clear the current call and choices from the app, or close the tab to end the session. Your original audio file and any reports you downloaded remain on your device. Browser cache and download history are managed separately through your browser settings.

05 / Reports you save or share

Save report downloads a JSON file to your device. It can include transcript text, warning evidence, scheme labels, speaker labels, recording-quality results, checklist choices, and feedback notes. It does not embed the audio recording.

Review a report before showing it to others; it may contain private conversation details. You control where you store, share, or delete the downloaded file. Cue does not automatically send it anywhere.

06 / Recordings involving other people

Only use recordings you are entitled to process and for which you have any required consent. Avoid entering passwords, account numbers, or other unnecessary personal information. Sample transcripts and the included synthetic audio are available for demonstrations.

07 / Questions and future changes

For questions about this local demonstration, contact the person who provided it to you. There is no in-app support inbox or account-data request service in this prototype.

If the app's data handling changes, this policy should be updated and dated to describe the new behavior. This policy describes the current local version, not a future hosted service or live-call app.
