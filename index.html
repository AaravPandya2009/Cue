      STAY IN CONTROL

      A warning is useful.
      A next step is better.

      You don’t have to decide while someone is on the line.

      Read the FTC’s phone-scam guide ↗
      01

      Pause the conversation.

      If a caller pressures you, hang up. Take time to check their story before acting.

      02

      Verify on your terms.

      Contact the organization using a number from its official website or your card. Don’t rely on caller ID.

      03

      Keep control of your accounts.

      Don’t give unexpected callers passwords, verification codes, payment, or remote access.

      BEHIND THE ASSESSMENT

      Four lenses.
      One clearer picture.

      No single signal tells the whole story.

      01

      Language & tone

      Looks for pressure and threat language in the transcript. It does not infer emotion from someone’s accent or voice.

      02

      Speaking pace

      Estimates words per minute from the transcript and recording length. Pace is context, and does not increase the risk score.

      03

      Requested actions

      Flags requests involving passwords, codes, unusual payments, and remote access using transparent language rules.

      04

      Reliability cues

      Highlights identity claims and discouraging verification. A recording cannot authenticate the caller.
